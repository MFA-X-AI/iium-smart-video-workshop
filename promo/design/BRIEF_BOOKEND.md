# Brief: one bookend still for the end of the promo

The promo opens on `shot-shoulder.png`: a tired lecturer at 2 a.m., over his right shoulder, fighting a slide
editor. The END of the promo returns to the same shot, but now **the computer is doing the work on its own** and he
is relaxed. Generate ONE still, `shot-bookend.png`, exactly 1536 x 1024 (3:2), with the built-in image generation
tool, using `variant-C.png` (approved identity) and `shot-shoulder.png` (approved framing and office) as references.

- Same lecturer, wardrobe, office, lamp, mug, bookshelf, night window as `shot-shoulder.png`. Same camera position
  and lens as `shot-shoulder.png` (from directly behind his right shoulder, looking past him to the laptop), so the
  two shots cut together as a bookend.
- New pose: he leans back in the chair, relaxed and satisfied, **both hands clasped behind his head, elbows out**,
  shoulders loose. His hands are NOT on the keyboard or mouse. His face is not visible (seen from behind), no
  reflected face.
- The laptop screen faces the camera, centred in the frame, large and unobstructed by his head or arms (his head
  can sit slightly left or right of it), a soft bright even glow with **no readable UI, glyphs or text** (I replace
  the screen content in the edit). Keep the screen fully visible including all four corners.
- A thin wisp of steam rises from the mug. Lighting: same warm copper lamp and cool teal laptop/night mix, a touch
  warmer and calmer than the opening.
- Photorealistic cinematic 35mm film still, shallow depth of field, subtle grain. One person only. No readable text
  anywhere, no logos, no watermark, blank lanyard card.

Save to `promo/design/shot-bookend.png` (copy it out of the generated-images folder if needed) and append the exact
prompt to `promo/design/NOTES.md`. Do not launch any browser. Write only inside `promo/design/`.
