# Design brief: the lecturer and his office (reference images)

For the opening of a promo video for an IIUM (International Islamic University Malaysia)
workshop on making research animations with AI. The opening shows "the old way": a tired
lecturer at 2 a.m. fighting a slide editor over a conference poster. These images are the
**design reference** for the character and set. After the user picks one, matching 16:9 stills
will be animated into short video shots with an image-to-video model (LTX-2.3).

## The man
- A Malaysian Malay man in his late thirties, a university lecturer. Relatable and a little
  rumpled, not a model: a kind, tired face, short neat beard, rectangular glasses.
- A light-blue long-sleeve office shirt, sleeves rolled up, top button open. A plain lanyard with a
  blank staff card (no logos or readable text).
- Consistent, distinctive, easy to recognise from behind or from the side too.

## The set
- A small, lived-in university lecturer's office at night. One warm desk lamp, an open laptop
  whose cool glow lights his face, stacks of journal papers and folders, a bookshelf, a window with
  dark night sky and distant city lights, a mug of coffee.
- Mood: cinematic film still, shallow depth of field, muted teal and warm amber, 35mm grain,
  photorealistic. Late-night quiet, a bit funny-sad, not bleak.
- No readable text anywhere, no logos, no watermarks. The laptop screen is a soft bright glow,
  never readable UI.

## Deliverables
Generate **3 design variants**, each a 16:9 landscape image (at least 1536 px wide). All show
the same scene (him at the desk, three-quarter view, laptop glow on his face), but vary his look
(face, beard, glasses, build) and the office's lighting and palette, so the user can choose. Save
them as `promo/design/variant-A.png`, `variant-B.png` and `variant-C.png` (copy them out of the
generated-images folder if needed). Then write `promo/design/NOTES.md` with the exact prompt used
for each, so the chosen one can be reproduced for the shot stills.
