# KICT — Eye corners estimate pupil spacing

## Paper and the so what
Read all eight pages of the supplied PDF, freshly extracted with uv run --no-project --with the locally cached pypdf wheel (the package index was unreachable). Confirmed against papers.json[10]. Extraction: clips/.qa/KICT-paper-fresh.txt.

**So what:** Existing face-landmark data can give an approximate estimate of the spacing between a person's pupils, even when the pupils themselves were never recorded.

The method uses 468 FaceMesh landmarks. Landmarks 33/133 and 263/362 define the eye corners; their midpoints are eye-centre proxies, not detected pupils. Mean eye-corner width and a Malay young-adult normative width of 29.100 mm establish the scale. Nested AutoML calibrates geometric features against reference PD. In 44 subjects, subject-level MAE improved from 4.26 to 3.510 mm. Wide agreement limits preclude replacing clinical measurements. The animation shows the geometric construction and symbolically signals calibration; it does not execute a trained model or display a participant measurement.

## Three concepts, before building
1. **A face assembles; four corners become two proxies and a distance.** A luminous scan binds a disordered cloud to a dimensional face. Four amber corner landmarks ignite. Signals travel from paired corners to their arithmetic midpoints. A spanning measurement grows between those proxies and receives a violet calibration sweep. Critique: the face is instantly legible, converging signals explain the calculation, and the connected distance makes a strong reveal. A frozen final frame explains the subject. Avoid pupil-like circles and never move proxy points during calibration.
2. **A wireframe face fits a pair of spectacles.** The eye span drives lenses into alignment. Critique: immediately human and visually strong, but implies clinical dispensing accuracy that the paper explicitly does not support. Reject despite a strong thumbnail.
3. **Two ghost faces merge as measurement error narrows.** An amber overestimated span settles into a cyan calibrated span while facial features register. Critique: a dramatic morph, but two faces are confusing in three seconds and an individual corrected span invents a case result. Aggregate MAE labels need too much reading. Reject.

**Selected: concept 1.** It makes the actual geometry the action, while a recognisable, densely sampled 3D face gives the shot scale and presence. Top-centred title: “Eye corners estimate pupil spacing” (five words). Cyan/ice-blue point clouds and triangular wireframe, amber canthi, violet calibration pass, midnight background. No panels, controls, legend, cursor or attribution. Eye action stays above the bottom-right citation reserve (x >= 940, y >= 780 at 1920×1080).

## Build plan
Embed the canonical 468-vertex MediaPipe face and triangles in one HTML file; no runtime model download. Canonical asset is a generic model, not a participant. Source: Google MediaPipe v0.10.21, mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj (Apache-2.0). Decorative surface samples are not extra detected landmarks. Pinned Three.js 0.169.0 ES modules and addons use jsDelivr. Deterministic three-second timeline, autoplay loop and exact ?t= stills.
