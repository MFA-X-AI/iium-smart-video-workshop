# KOS — touching the fibre

## Finding and source

Touching a glass bead to its optical fibre stores light much more effectively than leaving a small gap, supporting a more sensitive temperature sensor.

I read the supplied full author abstract in `../.qa/KOS/article.txt` and the proposal. The full PDF was unavailable. Source: Noorbahrin et al., *Results in Optics* 21 (2025), 100928, DOI [10.1016/j.rio.2025.100928](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rio.2025.100928).

The abstract reports fusion-splicer fabrication and two separately tested coupling configurations. Contact (0 µm): Q-factor 16,069.688, finesse 42.792. A 10 µm gap: Q-factor 659.997, finesse 1.111. The film uses only the gaps and Q-factors as numerical annotations. It does not attribute the abstract's temperature-sensitivity measurement to either configuration.

## Concepts considered before building

1. **Simultaneous pulse, two beads.** An identical pulse reaches two independent tapers; a sharp orbit builds in the touching bead while the other glow dies. The cause and effect remain visible together, and the final frame works as a thumbnail. Risk: glass and glow could conceal the small separation; use a precise bracket and restrained bloom. Selected: this follows the supplied staging and communicates storage in one shot.
2. **Open the gap during one exposure.** A bead lifts and its trapped light disappears. The action is immediately legible, but it could imply a measured transient or an experiment in which the gap was swept. It also loses the simultaneous comparison and conflicts with the required scene.
3. **Resonance sweep through two beads.** A travelling light sheet produces a narrow flash in one and a broad dim response in the other. This gestures at linewidth, but viewers would need an explanation of the sweep; a thumbnail would not tell the story. It risks inventing a measured spectrum.

## Scientific and visual licence

The sizes and proportions are illustrative; sphere diameter was not supplied. Following review of the supplied r1 renders, the drawn gap is exaggerated to **32 scene pixels**, approximately 9% of the revised 350-pixel bead diameter. It is not a scale bar. This licence is also explicit in the HTML comment. Side-by-side staging combines separately tested configurations. Warm gold is false colour for infrared light near 1543 nm. Seconds-long circulation, repeated lap buildup, emission strength, decay envelopes and camera movement are qualitative illustrations of energy storage, not measured lifetimes or a quantitative simulation of Q. Glass refraction and studio reflections are procedural artistic reconstructions. The two visible incoming arm lengths differ, so their illustrative travel speeds are normalized to show simultaneous arrival; they do not represent optical transit times.

The producer's disclosure is present from the first frame, directly under the title: **Illustration · real light is infrared · Q-factors as reported**. ## Validation

Built a single self-contained HTML file. Three.js 0.169.0 is imported through the pinned jsDelivr import map; there are no other network dependencies. A full-screen shader analytically intersects glass spheres, refracts at both dielectric interfaces, applies Fresnel studio reflections and subtle dispersion, and renders cylindrical fibre cross-sections. Broadening and dissolving the distant fibre segments, together with seeded defocused dust, give a procedural shallow-focus treatment. Analytic bloom is added only to emitted light. This is a custom optical illustration, not a measured ray-traced reconstruction of the apparatus.

For this final revision (r3), all writes, including validation scratch, stay inside `clips-v2/KOS/`. Passed `node --check clips-v2/KOS/.qa/module-check.mjs` after extracting the revised module.
