# KON — peak flow in clay

The finding: over one week, the asthma-exercise group had a larger mean peak-flow increase alongside usual medication (+176.33 L/min) than the medication-only group (+25.67 L/min). These are group means from a small, non-random study, not an individual promise.

Three concepts considered: (1) two clay patients push peak-flow sliders with their breath; (2) a clay lung inflates further with each exercise session; (3) three stepping stones raise an exercise figure above a medication figure. The lung is immediately familiar but could confuse peak flow with lung volume. The stepping stones communicate improvement but lose the measured quantity. The paired instruments are the strongest thumbnail and motion concept: the actual measurement instrument carries an honest, shared zero-based scale. This is also the concept prescribed by the brief.

## Design and implementation

Warm felt tabletop, teal and apricot cylindrical plasticine instruments, continuous sculpted profile faces with integrated puffed cheeks, and lips sealed onto rolled mouthpieces. The instruments have thick walls, a longitudinal slit, rounded collars and small clay supports. Stronger seeded bump maps, physical fingerprint ridges and uneven silhouettes make the clay texture visible at full resolution. Three warm shadow-casting lights approximate a broad key, with cool fill, distance falloff and a vignette. Rounded clay-coloured typography stays facing the camera; no downloaded font or image assets.

A perspective camera looks down at the tabletop from 37 degrees. The set is composed using calibrated screen anchors so both instruments retain the original linear 0–400 L/min scale and every slider centre retains its r1 screen position. The larger exercise figure wears a sweatband and sports shoes; its full body and a bevelled mat use the lower-left space. The mat's projected right edge is x=768. Meters and text remain above y=780; the bottom-right attribution region stays clear.

Pale ghost pins remain at both baselines. Coloured clay trails grow only from each baseline to its moving red indicator. One smaller control puff and three strong white exercise puffs first travel from the mouthpiece to the resting indicator, then remain in contact throughout each push. The larger puffs have clustered cotton lobes and a trailing wake. The exercise face visibly puffs more strongly. All object motion, text changes, breath and subtle surface wobble derive directly from time quantised to 1/12 s. All results are in place by 2 s, held through 3 s. A finite `?t=` freezes one deterministic frame; normal playback loops every 3 s. Three.js remains pinned to 0.160.0 on jsDelivr.
