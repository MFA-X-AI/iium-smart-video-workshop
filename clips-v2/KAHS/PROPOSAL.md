# KAHS v2 proposal: obesity lowers αvβ3-integrin in PCOS womb lining

Paper: Hamid FA, et al. (2025) "Obesity results in lower integrin expression in women with polycystic
ovarian syndrome during the window of implantation." Front. Endocrinol. 16:1590716. (7 pages, read fully.)

## What the paper did and found (with refs)
- 40 women, 4 groups of n = 10 (p.1, p.2): normal-weight controls (C-NW), obese controls (C-OB),
  normal-weight PCOS (P-NW), obese PCOS (P-OB). Endometrial biopsy in the window of implantation;
  RT-qPCR for αvβ3 integrin, mucin-1 and E-cadherin, 2^-ΔΔCt vs GAPDH (p.3).
- Table 2 (p.4): mean BMI P-NW 23.05 ± 1.46, P-OB 31.25 ± 1.62, C-NW 24.40 ± 1.94, C-OB 32.46 ± 1.53 kg/m².
- Results heading (p.3): "Obesity results in the downregulation of αvβ3 integrin gene expression in
  PCOS women": lower in P-OB than in P-NW and than in C-NW; P-NW vs C-NW no difference.
- Figure 1 (p.4), pairwise panels, the comparator group is set to 1.0 (no numbers printed on bars; values
  below are my pixel readings of the bar tops, ±0.01):
  - P-NW 1.0 vs P-OB ≈ 0.27, printed p = 0.090
  - C-NW 1.0 vs P-OB ≈ 0.30, printed p = 0.070
  - C-OB 1.0 vs P-OB ≈ 0.62, printed p = 0.330
  - C-NW 1.0 vs P-NW ≈ 1.01, printed p = 0.170
- Figure 2 (p.5): mucin-1 and E-cadherin, all 8 panels "no significant differences" (printed p 0.100-0.670).
- **Discrepancy to respect:** the text calls the integrin drop "significant" (threshold p < 0.05, p.3), but
  the p-values printed in Figure 1 for those comparisons are 0.070 and 0.090. So the clip must NOT say
  "significant". It shows the lower level as a measured difference in a small study (n = 10 per group),
  with the printed p-value in small type. Also, the Discussion's claim that obese non-PCOS women were also
  lower is not backed by any Figure 1 panel (no C-NW vs C-OB panel), so it is not used.
- Nothing about implantation success, embryos or pregnancy was measured. The clip shows expression only.

## Headline result
In women with PCOS, the obese group's womb lining expressed about a quarter as much αvβ3-integrin mRNA as
the normal-weight PCOS group (P-NW 1.0 vs P-OB ≈ 0.27 relative mRNA, Fig. 1, p = 0.090 as printed; n = 10
per group; mean BMI 23.05 vs 31.25 kg/m², Table 2), while mucin-1 and E-cadherin did not differ (Fig. 2).
Chosen because it isolates obesity within PCOS, which is exactly the paper's title and Results heading.

## Staging in the subject (not a chart)
A layered cut-paper diorama of two slices of womb lining (endometrium), side by side, like two pop-up
book pages. Each slice is built of stacked paper layers: deep rose stroma, a scalloped epithelial surface
of cut-paper cells, tiny gland loops cut out of the layers. On the surface, the αvβ3 integrin signal is a
row of **paper pop-up tabs** (small gold-ochre folded paper "Y"/fork-shaped receptor tabs, the
heterodimer suggested by two leaves) that fold up out of the surface with a paper-hinge motion.
- Both slices start flat. The tabs pop up across the surface, one after another, left to right.
- Left slice (normal weight): a full row pops up. Its ochre paper "fill strip" (a paper band tucked into
  the lining, like a level strip in a pop-up book) slides out to the full length marked "1.0".
- Right slice (obese): the same pop-up wave passes, but only about a quarter of the tab positions fold
  up (e.g. 4 of 15, i.e. the tab count ratio matches ≈ 0.27 in spirit; it is a visual proxy, see
  honesty notes); the rest stay flat, just cut outlines in the paper. Its level strip slides out only to
  ≈ 0.27 of the length and stops, with the label "≈ 0.27".
- The right slice's paper layers are visibly thicker/puffier in the fat layer beneath (a creamy
  adipose paper layer with round cut-out fat cells) to convey "obese" physically, since BMI 31.25
  differs from 23.05. (Artistic: the paper measured BMI, not endometrial fat.)
- Second beat (~2.0-2.6 s): two other small paper markers (mucin-1 as a lilac frond, E-cadherin as a
  teal zipper-like tab between neighbouring cells) are the same on both sides, and a small note says
  "mucin-1 · E-cadherin: no significant difference".
No bar chart, no axes. The "level strip" is a paper element inside the diorama, and the main carrier
is the number of raised tabs and the empty flat cut-outs on the obese side.

## Swap test
Only this paper compares αvβ3 integrin in obese vs normal-weight PCOS endometrium with these exact
numbers (BMI 23.05 vs 31.25, relative level 1.0 vs ≈ 0.27) while mucin-1 and E-cadherin stay unchanged.

## Visual style and medium
Layered cut-paper diorama: 3D (three.js) is appropriate, since depth by stacked paper layers, soft
contact shadows and a real light matter. Paper sheets as thin extruded shapes with procedural paper
fibre texture (canvas-generated noise, subtle deckled edges), warm key light from upper left, soft shadows
between layers, slight ambient occlusion feel. Palette: rose and blush endometrium layers, cream adipose
paper, gold-ochre tabs for integrin (the hero colour), muted lilac and teal for the unchanged markers,
warm off-white backdrop card. Motion language: paper hinges, tabs fold up with a quick overshoot and
settle like real paper; no morphing, no glow. Typography: labels as small cut-paper/letterpress style
tags pinned to the scene (a clean humanist serif), title in a confident serif on a pale paper scrim.
Why it serves the data: a pop-up book tab either stands or lies flat, so "fewer tabs rise" makes the
lower expression level legible at a glance, and the empty cut-outs make the missing expression visible.
