140 recent, openly readable papers from all 14 kulliyyahs, each rated for how well it would work as an AI-built animation. One top pick and one runner-up per kulliyyah, with a concrete idea for what moves and what the viewer controls.

Chosen for a clear mechanism or process, something the viewer can adjust, and a short build time. All 28 picks and runners-up were rechecked separately for title, IIUM kulliyyah, year and open access.

All 140 papers

Ten per kulliyyah, mostly 2026 with some 2025. Filter by kulliyyah or animation score, or search titles, departments and ideas.

How this list was made

  • Source: IIUM Repository (IRep) kulliyyah listings for 2025–2026, cross-checked with the publishing journals. Each paper has at least one author filed under that kulliyyah on IRep.
  • "Open" means a public PDF on IRep or a free full text on the journal site. Many IRep copies are restricted, so the link often points to the journal instead.
  • The 28 shortlisted papers were each reopened and checked separately. The other 112 were checked once while collecting; about half rely on the journal being open access without the article page being opened, so click through before using one.
  • Some papers are co-listed under two kulliyyahs, and some have IIUM staff as co-authors rather than lead authors. These are flagged on the picks.
  • Animation scores (1–5) are a judgement of visual potential, not of research quality. Low scores are mostly surveys, reviews and doctrinal analyses.
  • Not a citation ranking. The citation databases could not be reached from here, so this is a sample of recent open work, not the "most famous" papers.
